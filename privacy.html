Privacy Policy

Ice Lakes: Winter Species
Developer: Urmas Koort
Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ice Lakes: Winter Species (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is provided by Urmas Koort (“we”, “us”, “Developer”).

1. Overview

The App is an offline-first encyclopedia about ice fishing, lakes, and species. It does not require you to create an account. Core reference content is stored on your device. Some features use the internet (weather, community photos, configuration updates, and optional analytics).

2. Information we collect

On your device (local storage)

  • Favorites and quiz progress (stored locally on your device).
  • App settings such as theme, sound, and haptics preferences.
  • Cached configuration or session data needed for certain in-app experiences.

Sent to our or third-party services

  • Device and app identifiers (for example, an advertising/analytics identifier and app listing ID) may be sent to our configuration and analytics partners to measure installs and campaign performance, if you allow tracking when prompted.
  • Push notification token, if you enable notifications, may be sent so we can deliver notifications.
  • Interaction data related to notifications (for example, when you open a notification) may be sent for analytics.

We do not collect your name, email, or password through the App unless you contact us directly by email.

3. Third-party services

The App may use third-party SDKs and APIs, including but not limited to:

  • AppsFlyer — attribution and analytics.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — analytics and advertising-related features as configured in the App.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts for lake locations from bundled data (not your live GPS track).
  • iNaturalist — community photos and related metadata when you view species or lake galleries.
  • Remote image hosts (for example Wikimedia or URLs embedded in our content) when images are loaded.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their documentation.

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On supported iOS versions, the App may ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Your choice affects whether certain identifiers can be used for personalized advertising and attribution. You can change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Push notifications

If you grant permission, the App may send notifications. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings at any time.

6. Location

The App shows lake locations using coordinates included in bundled content and may open Apple Maps or Google Maps when you choose to navigate. We do not continuously collect your precise location in the background for this feature unless future versions explicitly request location permission and disclose it here.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Data retention and security

Local data remains on your device until you delete the App or reset app data. Data sent to servers or partners is retained according to their policies and our operational needs. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights or ask questions, contact us at the email below.

10. International transfers

Service providers may process data in countries other than your own. By using the App, you understand that data may be transferred to jurisdictions with different privacy laws.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
