Requirements

  • Platform: iPhone
  • Minimum iOS: 17.0

Common topics

  • Encyclopedia & quiz — Fish and lake data are bundled in the App; favorites and daily quiz progress are saved on your device.
  • Weather & ice estimates — Forecasts and thickness hints are informational only. Always verify ice safety locally before going on the ice.
  • Notifications — Manage in iOS Settings → Notifications → Ice Lakes.
  • Tracking permission — Manage in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Privacy — See our Privacy Policy.
